**Snapshot:** Digital commercial insurance marketplace. St. Petersburg, FL. Founded 2009. ~200+ employees. $250-500M revenue. $26M funding. Subsidiary of INSURICA. tivly.com

**Products:** (1) Commercial insurance lead generation, 70K+ connections/month, 1.4M businesses annually (2) Smart matching technology connecting SMBs to 350+ insurance providers (3) Digital optimization and call center services for carrier partners

**Primary buyer:** Insurance carriers and agencies needing SMB commercial distribution

**Go-to-market:** Digital marketplace + call center. Channel partnerships with carriers, agencies, MGAs.

**Problems solved:** (1) SMBs can't find the right commercial coverage quickly (2) Carriers struggle to reach small commercial customers efficiently (3) Agents need lead flow without building their own acquisition engine

**Value chain - upstream needs:** Address verification, business classification data, digital forms/application intake, compliance/licensing, rating engines, submission automation

**Value chain - downstream needs:** E-signatures for binding, payment processing, policy management, claims communication, billing/premium finance, renewal automation
