### What they sell
1. **Configurable P&C core platform (PAS):** policy admin, underwriting workbench, claims, agent portal. "Custom-platform power without mega-vendor lock-in — launch products in months, not years."
2. **Higson business-rules engine:** automates complex underwriting and pricing decisions.
3. **Insurance IT services:** custom development and integration with existing systems.

*Primary revenue driver:* core-platform / PAS modernization deals with carriers.

### Who they sell to
- **Primary buyer:** mid-tier P&C carriers ($500M–$5B GWP) running aging legacy cores.
- **Secondary:** specialty/MGA/E&S writers, fronting/reinsurance-heavy carriers, and US arms of Tier-1 global groups (modular add-ons).
- **Buying committee:** CIO / CTO / Head of IT · Head of Distribution/Sales · Head of Digital / Transformation or COO.

### Problems they solve
1. Aging COBOL/mainframe cores that block new products and digital front-ends.
2. Risky, expensive in-house modernization efforts that stall.
3. Growth or new D2C strategies outpacing the legacy system's limits.

### The prospect thesis (why these 52)
A carrier is a good Decerto target when a **legacy-core displacement window** is open: the core is old or being risk-rebuilt in-house, the carrier is big enough to fund a replacement but too small to have a modern PAS already, and something is forcing the issue now.
